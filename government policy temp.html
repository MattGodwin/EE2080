The Warm Homes: Social Housing Fund(WH:SHF)

 

What is the WH:SHF?

·       The WH:SHF is a government initiative that functions as the continuation of the previous Social Housing Decarbonisation Fund.

·       The WH:SHF is wave 3 of an overarching governmental decarbonisation plan.

·       The WH:SHF is aimed at elevating homes to an EPC C rating or higher.

·       The WH:SHF is also meant to address a growing issue of fuel poverty.

·       The WH:SHF has had £1.29billion allocated to it.

 

Which Homes are Eligible?

·       Social homes with EPC ratings below EPC C.

·       Social Homes above EPC C rating, once the plan includes a switch to low carbon heating and accounts for no more than 10% of the overall application.

 

Who Can Apply?

·       Local authorities

·       Housing Associations

·       Registered Charities that own social housing

·       Any registered Providers of social housing

 

What Retrofit Measures are Encouraged?

·       Loft insulation

·       Cavity wall insulation

·       Internal wall insulation

·       External wall insulation

·       Floor insulation

·       Draught-proofing

·       Ventilation upgrades

·       Solar Photovoltaic (PV)

 

What Low Carbon Heating Measures are Eligible?

·       Air-source heat pumps

·       Ground-source heat pumps

·       Shared ground loops

·       Heat network connections

 

What are the Stipulations?

·       There is a £7,500 base cost cap per home.

·       There is an off gas grid low carbon heating cost cap uplift that has an additional £7,500 of grant funding available if the Grant Recipient installs low carbon heating measures in homes off the gas grid.

·       All grant funding must be spent by 31/03/2028.

·       The delivery window for the projects ends on 30/09/2028

·       All works must follow PAS 2035:2023.

·       All installers must be TrustMark registered.

 

What to Consider With Residents?

·       Clear communication between the council and residents.

·       Minimal disruption to residents daily life.

·       Ensure retrofitting does not result in any bill increases.

·       Consideration and protection of particularly vulnerable residents.

 

Where to Apply?

Applications are done via the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero(DESNZ) through bidding competitions.
